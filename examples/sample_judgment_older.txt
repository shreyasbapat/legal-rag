IN THE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA

This illustrative text is for demonstrating the ingestion pipeline only
and is not a verbatim reproduction of the reported judgment.

1. The question referred was whether Parliament has the power under
Article 368 to amend Part III of the Constitution so as to take away or
abridge fundamental rights.

2. The majority held that fundamental rights are given a transcendental
position and are kept beyond the reach of Parliament, and that an
amendment under Article 368 is "law" within the meaning of Article 13(2).
