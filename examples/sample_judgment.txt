IN THE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA

This illustrative text is for demonstrating the ingestion pipeline only
and is not a verbatim reproduction of the reported judgment.

1. The petitioner challenged certain constitutional amendments on the
ground that Parliament's amending power under Article 368 is not
unlimited.

2. Reference was made to the earlier view in AIR 1967 SC 1643, which had
held that fundamental rights could not be abridged by constitutional
amendment.

3. The Court held that while Parliament has wide power to amend the
Constitution, that power does not extend to altering its basic
structure.

4. The decision in AIR 1967 SC 1643 was accordingly overruled to the
extent it held Article 368 could never be used to amend Part III.
